LOOP Lab

Learning through Organ Observations and Perturbations · 장기반응 모델링 연구실 · Graduate School of Data Science, Chonnam National University

We make the data our models are judged by.

A model that predicts how a drug will act is only as good as the measurements it learned from. Most of those come from single cell types on flat plastic.

We work on the side of that problem where the data is made. We build human tissue in microphysiological systems, run perturbations on it, and turn the result into data a model can be judged against.

Perturb

Human tissue built in microfluidic chips, then dosed across a concentration gradient.

Observe

Imaging, contractile force and transcriptomics, measured on the same tissue.

Learn

Readouts that reproduce, reduced to a matrix a model can be fitted to.

Predict

Estimates of drug response that decide the next experiment to run.

Join us

We are recruiting M.S. and Ph.D. students and undergraduate interns.

The work moves between growing cells at the bench and handling the data those cells produce. You are not expected to be proficient in both — starting from one side and being willing to cross over is enough. Backgrounds in mechanical engineering, life sciences, chemical engineering, computer science and statistics are all suitable.

If you came to data science to work on models, this is a place where you can also decide what the training data is.

Email jsang@jnu.ac.kr and tell me what you have been working on and what you would like to work on.
